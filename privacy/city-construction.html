Privacy Policy — City Construction

Last updated: 5 July 2026 · Published by Soft Gaming Tech

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game City Construction ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

The App is a single-player, offline construction-driving game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from you. Specifically, the App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any account information.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device only (for example: completed levels, star ratings, high scores, and sound/music settings). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — reserved for optional online features and future updates. The current version does not send personal data over the network.

4. Advertising

The current version of the App does not display third-party advertisements and does not use an advertising SDK. If advertising is added in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the advertising partners used (such as Google AdMob) and the data they process, before that version is released.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe any personal data has been provided to us, please contact us and we will address it.

6. Data security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data held by us to be lost or misused. Any locally stored game progress remains under your control on your device.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, within the App or its store listing.
